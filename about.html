About Innoventra

5 years of engineering excellence, shaping Bangladesh's built environment.

Our Story

Engineering Bangladesh's Future Since 2007

Innoventra Design and Engineering was established in Dhaka with a vision to transform Bangladesh's construction landscape through technical innovation, professional ethics, and client-focused service delivery.

Starting with a small team of 5 engineers, we have grown to a 200+ strong organization handling projects across residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure sectors. From RAJUK-approved Dhaka highrise apartments to Chittagong port infrastructure and Sylhet tourism complexes — Innoventra's signature is visible across the nation.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, sustainable, and cost-effective engineering solutions that contribute to Bangladesh's socioeconomic development — while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and professional integrity in every project we undertake.

Our Vision

To be Bangladesh's most trusted and technologically advanced engineering conglomerate — recognized regionally for building infrastructure that improves lives, drives economic growth, and stands the test of time for future generations.

Why Clients Trust Us

Our track record speaks louder than any claim. 98% client retention rate, zero structural failure record, and a portfolio spanning government, corporate, and private sectors — backed by BNBC compliance and ISO certifications.
