Our Services

Complete engineering and construction solutions for every project type across Bangladesh.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Engineering Solutions

From concept to completion — our specialized service divisions handle every aspect of your construction project.

Residential Construction

From single-family homes in Uttara to luxury apartment complexes in Banani — we build residences that blend aesthetics with functionality and BNBC-compliant structural integrity.

G+10 to G+20 highrise apartments
RAJUK-approved design documentation
Turnkey delivery with interior finishing

Commercial Construction

Office towers, shopping malls, hospitals, hotels, and mixed-use commercial complexes designed for maximum ROI, tenant appeal, and long-term durability in Dhaka's competitive market.

High-rise commercial towers up to 30+ floors
Retail malls and hospitality developments
LEED-aligned green building design

Industrial Construction

Garment factories, warehouses, EPZ facilities, cold storage units, and manufacturing plants engineered for industrial efficiency, fire safety, and compliance with Bangladesh Export Processing Zone Authority standards.

RMG factory design and construction
NFPA fire safety compliance
EPZ, BEPZA, and BEZA zone projects

Structural Engineering

Rigorous structural analysis and design using ETABS, STAAD.Pro, and SAP2000 — covering RCC frames, steel structures, pre-engineered buildings, and complex foundation systems for Bangladesh's soft-soil conditions.

BNBC 2020 and ACI code compliance
Seismic analysis (Zone II-III)
Pile foundation and retaining wall design

Design Consultancy

Expert architectural and engineering consultancy for project feasibility, master planning, BOQ preparation, tender support, and independent design review for government and private sector clients.

Feasibility and site analysis reports
BOQ and cost estimation services
Third-party design audit and review

Renovation & Retrofitting

Structural assessment and reinforcement of existing buildings — seismic retrofitting, HVAC upgrades, façade renovation, and adaptive reuse projects breathing new life into old structures.

Structural health assessment reports
Carbon fiber strengthening systems
Heritage building conservation

Turnkey Projects

Complete turnkey delivery from land acquisition to final handover — design, construction, MEP, interior, landscaping, and legal documentation handled by a single trusted partner. Ideal for developers and institutional clients seeking hassle-free delivery.

Single point of responsibility for entire project
Fixed-price contracts with performance guarantees
